The Rules

NON-NEGOTIABLE

Six rules. Rules 3 and 4 are the ones people break. Don't.

01
Code every day
One GitHub commit minimum. 147 consecutive days. The streak is the evidence.
02
Build before you watch
Every lecture is followed immediately by implementation. Watching is not building.
03
Miss a milestone — restart the phase
No milestone = restart that 21-day phase from Day 1. No extensions. No partial credit.
04
Deploy or it didn't happen
Every major project must be publicly accessible via URL. A notebook is not a deployment.
05
Log publicly
Your daily log lives on your public GitHub. Accountability by visibility is the mechanism.
06
Phases are sequential
Phase 04 is not survivable without Phase 03. The sequence is load-bearing. Do not disrupt it.
