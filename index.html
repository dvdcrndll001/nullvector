The Rules

NON-NEGOTIABLE

Six rules. They exist because the outcome requires them. Rules 3 and 4 are the ones people break. Don't.

01
Code every day
One GitHub commit minimum. 147 consecutive days. Sick, traveling, exhausted — you still push something. The streak is the evidence.
02
Build before you watch
Every lecture is followed immediately by implementation. Open a code editor before you press play. Watching is not building.
03
Miss a milestone — restart the phase
No milestone = restart that 21-day phase from Day 1. No extensions, no partial credit. The milestone is the gate. This is what makes the credential real.
04
Deploy or it didn't happen
Every major project is publicly accessible via URL before the phase ends. A local notebook is not a deployment. A product is something a stranger can use right now.
05
Log publicly
Your daily log lives on your public GitHub. Accountability by visibility is the most durable form of discipline there is.
06
Phases are sequential
Phase 04 is not survivable without Phase 03. Phase 05 is useless without Phase 04. The sequence is load-bearing. Do not disrupt it.
